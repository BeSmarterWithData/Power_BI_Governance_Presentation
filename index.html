The Core Tradeoff

Every report is connected: a strength and a risk

The benefit

Improve one shared model and every dependent report can benefit.

One fix. One definition. Reused across teams.

The risk

A breaking change in that same model can affect reports you do not own or even know about.

More reuse means more dependencies to understand.

The Hidden Risk

One small change. Many broken reports.

  • Change a calculation → unexpected results
  • Remove a referenced field → broken visual
  • Delete a used table → multiple dependencies fail
Reality
A change can break visuals or alter their meaning across workspaces. A chart that still renders can still tell the wrong story.
Illustrative impact: one shared object, many dependent reports.

From Risk to Informed Decisions

How do we manage this risk?

When structural model changes require report updates, the business needs to know where the affected measures and columns are used.

Know where it is used

Trace the model's measures and columns to dependent reports, pages, and visuals across workspaces.

Include indirect usage through other measures.

Make the right business decision

  • Decide whether the change is worth the impact
  • Coordinate required updates with report owners
  • Validate affected visuals before release
Visual-level lineage
Know what a change could affect before deciding how to make it.
